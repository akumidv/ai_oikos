# Agent — development

Project charter for the **development** agent: turn a task into a reviewed change; its roles are the modes it works in.

Agent definition: `agents/development.md` in the akmon standard. Read it at [`_aitna/akmon/agents/development.md`](_aitna/akmon/agents/development.md).

One section per role. Add project-specific scope, standing instructions, and known pitfalls under
the role they belong to; the akmon definition stays the single owner of its contract — do not restate
it here.

## review

assess what *is* — architecture, risk, trade-offs, conformance; a findings report.

Role of record: `roles/review.md` in the akmon standard. Read it at [`_aitna/akmon/roles/review.md`](_aitna/akmon/roles/review.md).

## architect

design what *should be* — options, contracts, docs, decision records.

Role of record: `roles/architect.md` in the akmon standard. Read it at [`_aitna/akmon/roles/architect.md`](_aitna/akmon/roles/architect.md).

## engineer

realize a decided structure in code, with tests.

Role of record: `roles/engineer.md` in the akmon standard. Read it at [`_aitna/akmon/roles/engineer.md`](_aitna/akmon/roles/engineer.md).
